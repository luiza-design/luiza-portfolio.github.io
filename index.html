Beyond the screen

I’m someone who enjoys calm surroundings. I’m not particularly drawn to loud events or crowded places; I feel most comfortable in peaceful spaces where I can relax and enjoy the moment.

I love nature and, whenever I can, I like to escape into it, listen to the birds and breathe fresh air. Spending time surrounded by nature helps me slow down and appreciate the simple things.

I have a strong love for exploring the world and have visited more than 20 European cities. Paris is one of my favourite places. I love its historic architecture, elegant buildings, charming narrow streets, beautiful facades, little cafés and the feeling of discovering something special around every corner. The city has a timeless atmosphere that makes even a simple walk feel memorable.

I also love food. Armenian cuisine will always be my favourite, but I enjoy Chinese cuisine too. I like preparing different dishes, experimenting in the kitchen and discovering new flavours.

Friendship is an important part of my life. I believe it is better to have a small circle of genuine, honest friends than a large number of superficial connections. For me, trust and sincerity matter most.

I’m an animal lover and have a dog whom I treat like a little child. I believe that if someone cannot give a pet the care, attention and affection it needs, it is better not to keep one. Animals, especially dogs, need warmth, attention and closeness just as people do.

I consider myself goal-oriented. When I have an idea in mind and truly want to achieve it, I keep working towards it, even when it seems difficult. I believe determination is one of my strongest qualities: if I really want something, I do my best to make it happen.

How I approach my work

I have more than one year of hands-on Shopify experience building and improving stores from scratch. My work includes Shopify store setup and customization, product and collection organization, landing pages, product pages, theme sections, custom Liquid, HTML and CSS customization, responsive layouts, and fixing smaller store issues.

I can help organize products and collections, maintain clean and structured store content, and improve the overall shopping experience. I also understand the importance of customer communication, handling inquiries, resolving issues and keeping workflows organized.

My background in psychology helps me think about how customers interact with a store, what builds trust, and how navigation and content can make the customer journey feel more natural. I aim to create stores that are visually consistent, easy to use, trustworthy and structured to support conversions.

My goal is to combine Shopify's technical side with an understanding of customer behaviour—balancing clean design, functionality, usability and a consistent brand identity across desktop and mobile.
