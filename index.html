The person

I’m Luiza Sargsyan — Armenian, based in Yerevan, and someone who believes in building a life filled with experiences worth remembering.

▤⌁⌂

I love travelling, discovering new places and collecting memories. I have visited more than 30 European cities, and Paris remains one of my favourites for its architecture, historic streets and atmosphere.

Freelancing gives me more than an income. It gives me freedom — to choose how I work, where I work, how I use my time and which direction I take.

I have worked in a traditional 9-to-6 environment, but I have always wanted to keep learning and building something of my own.

I enjoy reading, writing down my thoughts, trying new recipes and cooking Armenian food for people I love. I believe life is too short to postpone the things that matter. I want to look back with memories, not regrets.

My first profession was psychology, which helps me understand communication, customer behaviour and the way people interact with digital products.

I have more than a year of hands-on Shopify experience and have worked with Armenian and international clients, both through Upwork and independently.

I build and customize storefronts with a focus on visual clarity, responsive layouts, usability and the details that help a brand feel trustworthy.

My work includes Shopify store setup and management, theme customization, Liquid, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, custom sections, product and collection pages, landing pages, metafields, metaobjects, navigation, mega menus, Markets, currencies, shipping settings, SEO, app integrations, bug fixes and UX improvements.
