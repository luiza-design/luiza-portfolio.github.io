THE PERSON
BEHIND THE WORK

I’m Luiza Sargsyan — Armenian, based in Yerevan, and someone who believes in building a life filled with experiences worth remembering.

I’m 28, I love my life, and I want to live it in a way that gives me plenty to remember when I’m older.

I love travelling and discovering new places. I have already visited 30+ European cities, and travelling is one of the things that constantly gives me new perspectives.

My favourite place is Paris. I love its architecture, narrow historic streets, old buildings and the atmosphere that makes even an ordinary walk feel memorable.

Freelancing, for me, is not only about income. It is about freedom — freedom of choice, time, location, speech, spirit and the ability to make my own decisions.

Before freelancing, I worked in the government system with a traditional 9–6 schedule. I have always wanted to keep learning, growing and educating myself, and freelancing opened the door to continuous learning, international clients and a much more independent way of working.

Outside of work, I have quite a few things that make me feel like myself.

I love cooking — but only when I’m in the mood for it. And when I do cook, I especially enjoy making something for the people I love. I have a particular soft spot for Armenian cuisine.

I also love experimenting with new recipes. There is something satisfying about starting with an idea and turning it into something real with your own hands.

Another thing I genuinely enjoy is reading.

“The mind is a wonderful servant, but a terrible master.”

— Robin Sharma, The Monk Who Sold His Ferrari

I also write. I like putting my thoughts into words, creating something from an idea and eventually giving that thought to someone else. For me, writing is another way of creating.

I have big expectations from this life, and I want to live it that way every day.

I don’t want to look back one day and regret the things I didn’t do. Life is too short to spend it constantly worrying about things that will not matter in the end.

I want to enjoy the ordinary days too — the small moments, the people I love, the places I discover, the things I create and the experiences that become memories.

I want to live every day as if it matters.

WHAT
I DO

Shopify Specialist & Designer focused on creating clean, premium and conversion-conscious digital experiences.

SHOPIFY DESIGN LIQUID CRO UX

My first profession is psychology, and that background continues to influence the way I work today. It taught me how to communicate, understand people's needs and look for practical solutions to problems.

I have more than 1 year of hands-on Shopify experience , building and improving stores from scratch.

Over this time, I have worked on many successful projects — both through Upwork and outside Upwork, with Armenian clients as well as clients from abroad.

My work is not limited to simply setting up a Shopify store. I focus on the entire digital experience — how the store looks, how it works, how customers navigate through it and what helps them move toward a purchase.

I also work with Custom Liquid, HTML and CSS when Shopify's standard theme settings are not enough to achieve the exact design or functionality a project needs.

I work inside Edit Code when a project requires deeper customization — from small styling adjustments to custom sections, templates, components and functionality.

I care about the details that are easy to overlook: spacing, typography, responsive behaviour, navigation, product presentation, customer flow and the small interactions that make a store feel polished.

My psychology background is especially useful here. I don't only ask whether something looks good — I also think about how a customer will perceive it, understand it and interact with it.

My goal is to create Shopify stores that feel clean, premium, trustworthy and easy to use while also being structured with conversion in mind.
