How I approach my work

I have more than one year of hands-on Shopify experience building and improving stores from scratch. My work includes Shopify store setup and customization, product and collection organization, landing pages, product pages, theme sections, custom Liquid, HTML and CSS customization, responsive layouts, and fixing smaller store issues.

I can help organize products and collections, maintain clean and structured store content, and improve the overall shopping experience. I also understand the importance of customer communication, handling inquiries, resolving issues and keeping workflows organized.

My background in psychology helps me think about how customers interact with a store, what builds trust, and how navigation and content can make the customer journey feel more natural. I aim to create stores that are visually consistent, easy to use, trustworthy and structured to support conversions.

My goal is to combine Shopify's technical side with an understanding of customer behaviour—balancing clean design, functionality, usability and a consistent brand identity across desktop and mobile.

“Your next level begins with the decision to grow.” Stay curious. Keep building. Keep going.
Five principles to grow by
01

Keep learning your field

Study new tools, platform updates, design patterns, and the skills clients are asking for. Your expertise should keep evolving.

02

Research different markets

Follow specialists and businesses in different countries. Compare how markets work, what customers expect, and which solutions perform well.

03

Stay motivated and curious

Make room for new ideas, ask questions, and explore unfamiliar approaches instead of repeating what you already know.

04

Practice by building

Turn what you learn into real projects. Test ideas, solve problems, review the results, and improve your process with every iteration.

05

Communicate and deliver reliably

Set clear expectations, ask thoughtful questions, respect deadlines, and keep clients informed. Trust is part of the work.

A practical guide for freelancers

What You Need to Know
When Working on Upwork

U P W O R K

Five essentials for a stronger profile
1

Choose a Clear, Specific Professional Title

If your title simply says “Shopify Developer,” a client may not understand your specialisation, the services you provide, or the problems you can solve. Use your title to communicate your professional direction and the value you offer.

A more specific example Shopify Developer | Store Development & Customization | Custom Liquid, HTML & CSS
2

Build a Relevant Portfolio

Your portfolio should demonstrate your experience, skills and the quality of your work. It helps clients understand what you can offer and why they should choose you.

A strong project presentation should include:

Problem What challenge did the project have?
Solution How did you approach and solve it?
Result What changed or improved after your work?
3

Write a Targeted Profile Description

Your description should clearly explain what you do, which problems you solve and what value you can bring to a project.

Avoid generic wording “I am a hardworking and passionate Shopify developer. I always deliver high-quality work.”
Make it more specific “I specialize in Shopify store development and theme customization, including custom Liquid and CSS solutions, with a focus on responsive design and user experience.”
4

Customize Every Proposal

Make every proposal relevant to the specific project:

Address the client's actual requirements.
Explain how you plan to solve the problem.
Show relevant experience or a work sample.
Connect your approach to the client's needs.

Sending the same proposal to every client is a common mistake. It can make it seem as though you have not studied the project or understood the client's requirements.

5

Keep Your Profile Active and Up to Date

Completing your Upwork profile once and leaving it unchanged for months is not the best approach. Keep it aligned with your professional development.

Update your portfolio after completing new projects.
Add skills as you gain experience.
Refresh your service descriptions as your expertise grows.

Your Upwork profile should grow with you.
