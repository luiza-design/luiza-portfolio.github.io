ABOUT ME

01
PERSONAL

THE PERSON
BEHIND THE WORK

I’m Luiza Sargsyan — Armenian, based in Yerevan, and someone who believes in building a life filled with experiences worth remembering.

I’m 28, I love my life, and I want to live it in a way that gives me plenty to remember when I’m older.

I love travelling and discovering new places. I have already visited 30+ European cities, and travelling is one of the things that constantly gives me new perspectives.

My favourite place is Paris. I love its architecture, narrow historic streets, old buildings and the atmosphere that makes even an ordinary walk feel memorable.

Freelancing, for me, is not only about income. It is about freedom — freedom of choice, time, location, speech, spirit and the ability to make my own decisions.

Before freelancing, I worked in the government system with a traditional 9–6 schedule. I always wanted to keep learning and educating myself, and freelancing opened the door to continuous learning, international clients and a much more independent way of working.

02
PROFESSIONAL

WHAT
I DO

Shopify Specialist & Designer focused on creating clean, premium and conversion-conscious digital experiences.

SHOPIFY DESIGN LIQUID UX CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

My first profession is psychology, and that background continues to help me in my work. It taught me how to communicate, understand people's needs and look for practical solutions to problems.

I have more than 1 year of hands-on Shopify experience, building and improving stores from scratch.

I work with Shopify store setup and customization, product and collection pages, landing pages, product pages, HTML, CSS and Liquid customization, UX improvements and small technical fixes.

I have worked on many successful projects — both through Upwork and outside Upwork, with Armenian clients as well as clients from abroad.

My focus is always on creating clean, premium and trustworthy experiences: easy navigation, strong visual structure, clear customer flow and a store that feels professional from the first interaction.

I see Shopify not only as a technical platform. I also look at the experience from the customer's perspective — what they see, what they understand, where they hesitate and what helps them move forward.
