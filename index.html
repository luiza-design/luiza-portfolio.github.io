02 / Professional

Design

Shopify Specialist & Designer focused on creating clean, premium and conversion-conscious digital experiences. I work on store setup, theme customization, product pages, custom Liquid, CSS and responsive design, balancing a distinctive visual identity with practical functionality and a smoother customer journey.

A little more about me

I enjoy calm environments much more than crowded or noisy events. I appreciate having space to think, recharge and enjoy the moment without too much noise around me.

Nature is one of the things I genuinely enjoy. I love spending time outdoors, listening to birdsong and breathing fresh air. Simple, quiet moments often mean more to me than busy places.

I have visited more than 20 cities across Europe. Paris has a special place in my heart because of its historic architecture, beautiful buildings and streets that feel full of character.

I love cooking, especially Armenian and Chinese food. I also enjoy trying new dishes and flavours and discovering different ways of preparing familiar ingredients.

I value having a small circle of genuine, sincere friends. For me, the quality of a connection matters more than the number of people around me.

I love animals and have a dog whom I treat like a small child. Animals need care, attention and affection, and I believe those responsibilities are part of loving them.

I am goal-oriented and willing to do difficult things to reach my goals. When I decide that something matters to me, I try to stay consistent and find a way forward, even when the process is not easy.

How I work

I have more than one year of hands-on experience working with Shopify stores, helping businesses build, improve and maintain digital storefronts that reflect their brand and serve their customers.

My work includes Shopify store setup, product and collection pages, landing pages, theme customization, product uploads and organization, custom Liquid, HTML and CSS, responsive layouts, and updates to existing storefronts.

I focus on the relationship between design and functionality. A visually attractive store should also be easy to navigate, clear to understand and comfortable to use on both desktop and mobile.

My background in psychology helps me think about customer behaviour, trust and the way people interact with digital experiences. I pay attention to content hierarchy, navigation, consistency and the details that can make a customer journey feel more natural.

I can work with existing themes and customize sections to meet specific design requirements while preserving the store’s existing functionality. My goal is to create a professional, cohesive result that balances the brand’s visual identity with practical business needs.
